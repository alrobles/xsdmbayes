# xsdmbayes: A Demographic Approach to Species Distribution Model

Integrates concepts of stochastic demography into species distribution
modelling. Inferences, utilizing Bayesian methods based in 'Stan', are
made of aspects of the environmental niche of a species, and those
inferences can be used to project species geographic range. Data
requirements include species detection records and spatiotemporal
environmental data in Berti et al. (2024,
[doi:10.1101/2024.10.30.621023](https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.10.30.621023)
) for a description of the statistical and demographic models used.
